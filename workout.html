Weekly Knee + IT Band Prevention Plan — Race Prep Edition
Goal unchanged: pelvic control + knee tracking under fatigue. Mode changed: maintenance, not building. Loads drop to ~70%, Saturday goes bodyweight-only so Sunday long runs start on fresh stabilizers.
Race Countdown — how this plan winds down
🗓 Final 4 weeks
  • Now → ~2 weeks out: run this reduced plan as written. All weights ~70% of what you've been lifting — you're maintaining, not building.
  • ~10 days before the race: last session with any weights or machines. After that, bodyweight and bands only.
  • Race week: Saturday-style activation circuits only (band walks, bridges, leg swings). Nothing that leaves you sore.
  • Every week: Saturday stays bodyweight-only — the loaded glute-med work now lives on Tuesday, three days clear of the long run.
